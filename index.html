Case study: JustBaby Luv

A Shopify e-commerce brand selling a smart diaper bag. I handled SEO end to end: audit, structured data, content, digital PR and automation.

The starting point

An audit of justbabyluv.com covered PageSpeed, Core Web Vitals, indexing health, internal linking and AI citation. The brand scored 1/100 for AI citations, with invalid Product schema and missing SEO titles holding it back.

What I did

  • Fixed Product and Merchant listing schema in the Shopify theme (image, description, brand) and validated it in Search Console
  • Added JSON-LD with sameAs links so AI engines recognise the brand as one entity
  • Diagnosed indexing problems, including a 401 error blocking Googlebot and broken sub-sitemaps
  • Ran a Semrush backlink gap analysis against competitors and built an n8n backlink automation pipeline
  • Tracked AI visibility with Otterly and Semrush

The result

Users grew from 115 to 500 in July (+335%), then from 500 to 800 in August (+60%): about 7x in three months.

AI citation rate improved by roughly 70% across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity.
